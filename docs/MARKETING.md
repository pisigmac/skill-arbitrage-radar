# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Audience
- Freelancers earning $30-80/hr looking to specialize
- Developers wanting to pivot to higher-paying niches
- Career switchers with transferable skills
- Agencies looking for niche expansion data

## Pricing Tiers
- **Free** - Public arbitrage listings, basic stats
- **Pro ($29/mo)** - Personalized recommendations, portfolio generator, outreach templates
- **Enterprise ($99/mo)** - API access, team features, custom scrapers

## Launch Plan
1. **Week 1** - ProductHunt launch, Hacker News post
2. **Week 2** - LinkedIn content series on skill arbitrage
3. **Week 3** - Partner with freelance coaches for affiliate
4. **Week 4** - Paid ads on Twitter/X targeting freelancers

## Key Messages
- "Stop competing on price. Start competing on scarcity."
- "The best freelance niche is the one nobody knows about yet."
- "Data-driven skill specialization."

## Channels
- Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit (r/freelance, r/webdev)
- ProductHunt, Hacker News, IndieHackers
- Newsletter sponsorships (Pointer, TLDR)
